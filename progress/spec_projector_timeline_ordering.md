## The rule, in one paragraph, so the table below has something to refer to

Sort `events` by `occurredAt` ascending. Within a **tie group** — the entries sharing one identical `occurredAt` — sort by **causal depth** ascending, then by `eventId` ascending. An entry's cause is the entry of its own tie group whose `eventId` equals its `causationId`; depth is `0` when there is none and `1 + depth(cause)` otherwise, evaluated over at most `|G|` rounds. `causationId` is already on every envelope reaching the projector; it is simply not stored on the entry today. Storing it is the whole mechanism.

## The two things I would look at first

1. **Open point 2.** A1 does not, on its own, order the `payment.received.v1` / `credit.released.v1` pair, because they are siblings and not a chain. If the amendment is approved without the Billing follow-up, the completion timeline goes from *deterministically wrong* to *arbitrary* — better, and still not right. I want that trade named out loud rather than discovered at the next review.
2. **Open points 4 + 6 together.** Whether `causationId` is on the wire decides whether the black-box suite can assert the ordering rule generally or only per scenario — and a per-scenario assertion is the same kind of hand-maintained artefact that open point 1 rejects.
