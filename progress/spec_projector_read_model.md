## Notes for the gate

- **Row 2 is the one to read first.** It is the only decision here that #8 and #9 inherit verbatim as a *design* choice, and it is a deliberate extension of a rule in `specs/shared/` ("Kafka carries facts, NATS carries RPC"). The mitigation is that the signal has no responder, no reply subject and is never awaited, so the rule's *purpose* is untouched — but the extension is real and should be a decision, not a drift. The declined branch is fully specified and costs one topic.
- **Rows 1 and 3 are the two that touch things outside this feature.** Row 1 is one line in `apps/seed` plus a manual index drop; row 3 is two rows in `specs/shared/test-matrix.md`. Both are trivially revertible; both are load-bearing if declined (row 1 breaks `R53` outright, row 3 makes feature 24 unclosable under a literal reading of matrix rule 3).
- **Rows 8 and 9 are the two most likely to bite in implementation.** Row 8 is the hazard the brief named, and it is answered structurally rather than by care: `PR6` forbids the read-then-write shape, and task **G5** proves the absence of the read by recording the driver's commands. Row 9 was **not** in the brief and was found while reconciling against `apps/seed` — replaying against an already-seeded database is a live `R51`+`R52` violation that no clean-database test can reach.
- **Row 7 matters to the harness, not just this feature.** OI12's variant branch has been dormant since it was built; this is its first subject, and task **D7** requires the `N5` mutation to be re-run against it and recorded. If that probe does not fail, the guard is still text-only and the leader should hear about it before the feature closes.
