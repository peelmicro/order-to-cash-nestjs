| 2 | **How are "active holds" and "open invoices" computed from an append-only ledger?** `domain-model.md` §5.1's formula uses the qualifier *"release applied to holds"/"applied to exposures"*, which no entry's fields can answer — and read naively it yields a **negative** `openExposure` for an order cancelled before invoicing, breaking **B5**. | The two-term identity: `exposure(order) = Σhold − Σrelease`; `openExposure = min(Σconsume, exposure)`; `activeHold = exposure − openExposure`; `availableCredit = creditLimit − (Σ_line hold − Σ_line release)`. Exact, needs no qualifier, and makes `R40`'s neutrality a **property of the formula** rather than a rule applied on top. Net-sum over the ledger; **no projection, no materialised column, no migration**. | `design.md` §3.3 (the crux), `BC5`, `BC6`; rejected alternatives in §12 |
