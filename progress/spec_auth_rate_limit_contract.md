## Correction to the brief's premise, before anything else

The brief states there is "no `headers:` block anywhere in the entire document — grep confirms zero", and asks me to establish the house pattern. **That is not what the file contains.** `grep -n "headers:"` returns six hits: a `components.headers` section (line 967, holding the reusable `XCorrelationId`) and five in-path response `headers:` blocks — `POST /orders` 201 (`Location`, `X-Correlation-Id`), **`GET /orders/{id}` 202 (`Retry-After`)**, `GET /orders/stream` 200 (`Cache-Control`, `Connection`), and `POST /invoices/{id}/payments` 201 and 200 (`X-Correlation-Id`, `Idempotent-Replay`).

The third of those is decisive: **this contract already declares a `Retry-After` header**, on the projection-pending `202`, as

```yaml
            Retry-After:
              description: Suggested seconds to wait before retrying.
              schema:
                type: integer
                minimum: 0
```

So there was no pattern to establish — there was a pattern to **follow**, and following it was clearly right: two `Retry-After` declarations in one contract that disagreed about type or units would be worse than either choice on its own. Everything in open points 2–4 is therefore a decision about how far to deviate from an existing precedent, not a green-field shape decision. I would rather report this than quietly accept a premise and claim credit for inventing what was already there.

## What I wrote, in one paragraph each

**`openapi.yaml`.** `TooManyRequests` keeps its `Problem` body, gains a fuller `description` (the request was not applied, no fact was emitted, retry after the named interval) and a `headers:` block declaring `Retry-After` as `required: true`, `type: integer`, `minimum: 0`, `examples: [60]`, with a description fixing the unit as whole seconds, excluding RFC 9110's HTTP-date form, and stating explicitly that the window length itself is per-assessment configuration. No `R<n>` is cited in the OpenAPI text, deliberately — the header edit stands on its own even if the gate refuses R63.

**`requirements.md`.** A new **§8.1 "Edge protection of the unauthenticated login endpoint — R63"**, holding a three-paragraph filing note and one compound requirement. R63 requires (a) that a rate limit exists on the unauthenticated login endpoint published in `openapi.yaml`, (b) that tripping it yields `429` + `Problem` + `Retry-After` in whole seconds and issues no token, (c) that the refusal is independent of whether the submitted credentials were valid, and (d) that the limit is scoped to the login endpoint — a client refused there is still served everywhere else. It fixes no attempt count, no window length and no tracking key. Also updated: the requirement index (new row, total 62 → 63), §9's "these 62 requirements" and its coverage table (new *Abuse resistance at the edge* row), and §10's auth bullet, which now records the one exception rather than reading as a blanket exemption.

**`test-matrix.md`.** Title range `R1`–`R62` → `R1`–`R63`; a coverage-summary row (`8.1 … | R63 | 1 | 0`, total 63 rows, green still 46); a new §8.1 group with one `R63` row at **API** level naming three cases in `api/login-rate-limit.spec`; the *Verification* count and its `R61`/`R62` next-free-id note extended to R63; and a new *Abuse resistance at the published edge* row in the cross-cutting demonstrations table. **The row is `TODO`, not `DONE`** — see open point 11.
