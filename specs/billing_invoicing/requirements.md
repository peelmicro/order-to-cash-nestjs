# `billing_invoicing` — Requirements (assessment #7)

> **The normative requirements for this feature are `R45` and `R46` in [`specs/shared/requirements.md`](../shared/requirements.md) §6**, elaborated by [`specs/shared/domain-model.md`](../shared/domain-model.md) §5.2 (the `Invoice` aggregate, the `InvoiceLine` child entity, invariants **B6** – **B9**) and §5.3 (the invoice state machine), §5.1 (the `consume` ledger entry `R40` writes), §7.1 (the envelope) and §7.2 fact 10 (`invoice.issued.v1`); [`specs/shared/saga.md`](../shared/saga.md) §2 (the command vocabulary and its idempotency keys), §3.1 steps 4 and 5, §5 (the consumption map — Billing consumes **no** fact) and §6 (the redelivery table and the three idempotency layers); and [`specs/shared/asyncapi.yaml`](../shared/asyncapi.yaml) (the `billing.invoice.issue` / `billing.invoice.list` request and reply schemas, the `InvoiceIssued` message, the `billingFacts` topic). They are **not restated here** — this file adds only the local requirements (`BI<n>`) for gaps the shared spec leaves to each assessment, and the traceability for them.

> **`R47` – `R49` are NOT this feature's.** They are the remittance intake (feature 22, `billing_remittance_intake`) and stay `TODO` in the shared matrix. This feature owns the **aggregate shape** they need — `Invoice.markPaid`, the `paid` state, the `payment.received.v1` builder — plus the lock-ordering rule feature 22 must obey (`BI8`), so that feature 22 is an additive change rather than a redesign of this one.

> **`R46` is delivered but only half-driven.** `Invoice.markPaid` ships with its domain unit tests and **no live caller**, exactly as feature 19 shipped `BuyerCredit.consumeHold` and `releaseHold` for features 21 and 22. The `R46` matrix row is flipped here because its named test is a domain-unit test this feature writes and greens; nothing about remittance intake, the `payments` table or `credit.released.v1` is claimed. Per `CLAUDE.md` § Testing conventions and the `test-matrix.md` preamble, the fact-emission rule applies to `markPaid`'s `payment.received.v1` **with double force**, because no integration harness in this feature can reach it: the implementer arms the deletion of that emission itself and records which named test failed.

> **`R40` gets its first real caller here.** Feature 19 flipped `R40` on a domain-unit test of `BuyerCredit.consumeHold` with no caller. This feature is that caller. `R40`'s matrix row is **not** re-flipped (it is already `DONE`), but `BI7` and `BI17` below are the requirements that make the call correct, and the live-boot evidence of §5 is what makes it real.

## 1. Vocabulary local to this feature

| Term | Meaning here |
|---|---|
| **issue command** | One `billing.invoice.issue` NATS request, issued by the saga orchestrator after it has observed `order.despatched.v1` (`saga.md` §3.1 step 4). Never a fact, never a Kafka message. |
| **active hold** | `activeHold(orderReference) > 0` on the `(retailerCode, companyCode)` credit line, as `credit-exposure.ts`'s `summariseLedger` defines it (feature 19 design §3.3). **Billing's only proxy for "the order is despatched"** — see `BI5`. |
| **contract violation** | A defective or impossible request: no credit line, a currency that is not the credit line's, no active hold, a malformed payload. Answered with an `RpcError` reply, writing nothing and emitting nothing — the vocabulary the amended `R39` established (`specs/billing_credit/requirements.md` §3). |
| **domain outcome** | A legitimate business answer the caller must act on. **Invoice issue has exactly two**: `created: true` (a new invoice) and `created: false` (the idempotent repeat). There is no third — see `BI6`. |

## 2. Local requirements

### 2.1 The command surface and its refusals

**BI1.** THE SYSTEM SHALL issue an invoice **only** in response to a `billing.invoice.issue` RPC request, and the Billing service SHALL register **no** fact-stream consumer and **no** `order.despatched.v1` subscription of any kind — `saga.md` §5 gives Billing no consumed facts, and the orchestrator is the only component permitted to translate the despatch fact into the issue command (`saga.md` §3.1 step 4). IF a fact-stream consumer transport or an event-pattern handler is introduced into the Billing service, THEN the structural guard of `BI1` SHALL fail.

> **Why this requirement exists.** `feature_list.json`'s acceptance line for this feature reads *"issued on OrderDespatched"*, which reads like an instruction to subscribe to `order.despatched.v1`. It is not. The causal chain is `order.despatched.v1` → orchestrator → `invoice.issue` → `invoice.issued.v1`, and a Billing-side Kafka consumer would duplicate the trigger, bypass the saga's dedup record and the order-status precondition (`saga.md` §6 layers 1 and 2), and put a second, unsequenced writer on the invoice.

**BI2.** WHEN a `billing.invoice.issue` request arrives whose `x-correlation-id` or `x-request-id` header is absent or is not a valid `UniqueId`, THE SYSTEM SHALL answer with a `VALIDATION_FAILED` error reply **before** opening any transaction, SHALL create no invoice, SHALL append no ledger entry and SHALL emit no fact. The same rule applies to a payload that fails schema validation (an empty `lines` list, a non-positive `units`, a negative `unitPrice`, a malformed `orderReference`, a discount that exceeds the computed amount).

**BI3.** IF a `billing.invoice.issue` request names a `(retailerCode, companyCode)` pair for which no credit line exists, THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL answer `NOT_FOUND` naming the pair, SHALL create no invoice, SHALL append no ledger entry and SHALL emit no fact.

**BI4.** IF a `billing.invoice.issue` request names a `currency` other than the credit line's, THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL answer `VALIDATION_FAILED` carrying the expected and the received currency, SHALL create no invoice, SHALL append no ledger entry and SHALL emit no fact (a consequence of **M2** and of **B3**).

**BI5.** IF a `billing.invoice.issue` request names an order that holds **no active credit hold** on the named credit line — because the hold was refused, because a cancellation already released it, or because no hold was ever placed — THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL answer `PRECONDITION_FAILED` carrying the stable code `NO_ACTIVE_HOLD`, SHALL create no invoice, SHALL append no ledger entry and SHALL emit no fact.

> **Why `PRECONDITION_FAILED` and not an invoice.** Billing owns no order status and may not read the Orders write model (`domain-model.md` §1 boundary rule 1), so it cannot check `R45`'s literal precondition *"an order in status `despatched`"*. The active hold is the only order-scoped state Billing owns, and by the saga's construction every order reaching step 4 has one. Its absence therefore means the two write models disagree — a condition a retry cannot fix and a human must see. The saga dispatcher retries and then parks the row, which is exactly the desired handling.

**BI6.** THE SYSTEM SHALL emit **no fact other than `invoice.issued.v1`** in response to a `billing.invoice.issue` request. There is no invoice-rejection fact in the catalogue of `domain-model.md` §7.2, so every non-success outcome of this subject is an error reply and every success is either a new invoice with exactly one fact or an idempotent repeat with none.

### 2.2 The transaction, the ledger and the locks

**BI7.** WHEN an invoice is issued for an order holding an active credit hold, THE SYSTEM SHALL, **in one database transaction**, insert the invoice row and its line rows, append exactly one `consume` ledger entry of the order's active hold to the `BuyerCredit` aggregate, and record exactly one `invoice.issued.v1` outbox record; and IF any of those writes fails, THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL roll back all of them, leaving no invoice, no ledger entry and no outbox record, and SHALL answer with an error reply.

> **This transaction deliberately mutates two aggregates**, contrary to the default of `domain-model.md` §8 rule 6, and is the second such deviation in this repository (the first is `F3` in feature 17, where all-or-nothing reservation spans several `StockItem` aggregates). The justification is the same in shape: the alternative — two transactions, invoice then consume — leaves a crash window in which an invoice exists while its hold is still active, and **nothing can repair it**, because `R40`'s `consume` deliberately emits no fact (`asyncapi.yaml`'s `requestInvoiceIssue` description; feature 14's open point 5) and the saga can only compensate what it can observe. Making the window unrepairable-but-atomic is strictly better than making it observable by inventing a fourteenth fact, which would be a trilogy-wide contract change. Design §5.4 records the alternatives and why each was rejected.

**BI8.** THE SYSTEM SHALL acquire row locks in the Billing write model in exactly one order — `credits`, then `invoices`, then `invoice_number_sequences` — in every transaction of the service, present and future; and two concurrent `billing.invoice.issue` requests for the same order SHALL produce exactly one invoice, exactly one `consume` entry and exactly one `invoice.issued.v1`, with no deadlock and no lock-wait timeout.

> **Binding on feature 22.** `billing.payment.register` identifies its invoice by `invoiceReference`/`invoiceId` and must therefore read the invoice row **without a lock** to discover the `(retailerCode, companyCode)` pair, then take the `credits` lock, then re-read the invoice row `FOR UPDATE` and re-verify its status. Locking the invoice first would close a cycle with this feature's ordering and deadlock under concurrent payment and re-issue.

**BI9.** WHILE an invoice already exists for the `orderReference` named by a `billing.invoice.issue` request, THE SYSTEM SHALL answer with the existing `invoiceReference`, `invoiceDate`, `currency`, `totalAmount` and `status` and with `created: false`, SHALL write nothing at all — no second invoice, no second `consume` entry, no row of any kind — and SHALL emit no second fact (invariant **B7**, `saga.md` §6 layer 3). This holds whether the existing invoice is `issued` or already `paid`.

### 2.3 The aggregate

**BI10.** THE SYSTEM SHALL make an invoice whose `status` is `paid` without a `paidAt`, or whose `status` is `issued` with a `paidAt`, **unrepresentable in the domain layer**: the two are one value, not two independent fields; and IF a stored row is reconstituted whose `status` and `paid_at` disagree, THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL raise a domain error naming the invoice rather than construct the aggregate (invariant **B9**).

**BI11.** THE SYSTEM SHALL derive an invoice's `amount` as `Σ(unitPrice × units)` over its lines and its `totalAmount` as `amount − discount`, SHALL refuse construction when the line list is empty, when any line's `units` is not strictly positive, when a line's currency differs from the invoice's, or when `totalAmount` would be negative, and SHALL expose no way for a caller to assign any of the three (invariant **B6**).

**BI12.** THE SYSTEM SHALL allocate each `invoiceReference` as `INV-` plus a zero-padded six-digit sequence, unique across the service and never reassigned, using the same concurrency-safe counter-table mechanism `ORD-` and `DES-` use; and two concurrent allocations SHALL never yield the same value, and the first live allocation SHALL continue past the highest reference the seed already wrote rather than colliding with it.

**BI13.** THE SYSTEM SHALL stamp `invoice.issued.v1` with `aggregateId` = the invoice's own id, `correlationId` = the order id taken from `x-correlation-id`, `causationId` = the request id taken from `x-request-id`, and `occurredAt` = the invoice's `invoiceDate` read from the clock port — one fact, one builder, one call site.

**BI14.** WHERE feature 22 is not yet implemented, THE SYSTEM SHALL nevertheless provide `Invoice.markPaid`, which moves an `issued` invoice to `paid`, sets `paidAt` in the same indivisible step, and appends exactly one `payment.received.v1`; and IF `markPaid` is called on an invoice already `paid`, or with an amount or currency other than the invoice's `totalAmount` and currency, THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL raise a domain error, change nothing and append no event (invariants **B8**, **B9**, **B10**; shared `R46`, and the amount clause of `R49`).

### 2.4 The read side

**BI15.** WHEN a `billing.invoice.list` request is received, THE SYSTEM SHALL answer with a page of invoice views filtered by any supplied `status`, `retailerCode`, `companyCode` and `orderReference`, SHALL return only invoices whose `invoiceDate` is at least `issuedBeforeMinutes` minutes before the current time when that filter is supplied, SHALL read the current time from the clock port rather than from the ambient system clock, SHALL take no lock and open no transaction, and SHALL never mutate anything.

**BI16.** THE SYSTEM SHALL answer both new subjects with **bare JSON** — a plain reply object on success and a plain `RpcError` object on failure, with no framework packet envelope — and the subject constants SHALL equal the `address` of their AsyncAPI channels character for character.

### 2.5 Requirements inherited from earlier reviews

**BI17.** THE SYSTEM SHALL guard, mechanically and not by comment, that no amount reaching the credit-decision port from a Billing test fixture satisfies `amount mod 100 = 99` unless the fixture opts in explicitly; and the guard SHALL cover **computed** totals, not only literals, since a fixture of `3 × 8_333` produces `24_999` and no text scan of literals would see it. (`review_billing_credit_simulator.md` **N2**.)

**BI18.** IF `CREDIT_FAILURE_RATE` is set to a string that is not a plain decimal numeral — including `'0x1'`, `'1e0'` with an unintended exponent, or whitespace — THEN THE SYSTEM SHALL fail to start and report the offending value, rather than coerce it silently (`review_billing_credit_simulator.md` **N3**; strengthens shared `R43`'s last clause without changing it).

**BI19.** THE SYSTEM SHALL assert `R44`'s payload parity against the **observed** payload of the sibling simulated rejection rather than against a hard-coded key list, so that a key added on one path only makes the parity test fail (`review_billing_credit_simulator.md` **N1**).

**BI20.** THE SYSTEM SHALL NOT justify a retained class by naming a consumer that does not exist: `always-approve-credit-decision.ts`'s header SHALL claim only what is true — that it is the credit-decision port's reference implementation and the override a future harness may bind (`review_billing_credit_simulator.md` **N5**).

### 2.6 The order-total seam — flagged for the gate

**BI21.** THE SYSTEM SHALL make an invoice's `totalAmount` equal the order total for which the credit hold was placed: the saga SHALL populate the `invoice.issue` request's `discount` from the order's `initialDiscount`, and Billing SHALL compute `totalAmount = amount − discount`.

> **This is a change to `apps/orders`, and it is flagged for conscious approval** (`progress/spec_billing_invoicing.md`, row 5). Today `saga-command-payloads.ts` omits `discount` entirely, so an invoice's total is the order's **gross** `initialAmount` while the credit hold is on the **net** `totalAmount`. No seeded or live order has ever carried a non-zero discount, so the divergence is latent, not live — but `orders-create.dto.ts` accepts `lineDiscount` and `orderDiscount` today, the Gateway (feature 25) and the n8n order generator (feature 33) will both be able to send them, and `apps/seed/src/data/sagas.data.ts` already writes its own seeded invoices with `discount: initialDiscount`, which is the shape this requirement restores. If the gate declines, `BI21` is struck through and the divergence is recorded as owed.

### 2.7 The live steady state

**BI22.** WHEN the Billing service first boots with the `billing.invoice.issue` responder registered, THE SYSTEM SHALL cause every `invoice.issue` row parked in `saga_commands` to be re-dispatched by the existing sweeper, one invoice to be issued per order, one `consume` entry to be appended per order, one `invoice.issued.v1` to be published per order, and each of those orders to reach status `invoiced` — **and SHALL then stop there**, because `saga.md` §3.1 step 5 has the saga wait for the outside world: there is no internal timer, no polling and no further command until a remittance arrives through feature 22.
